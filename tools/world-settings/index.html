    Read this first

    Before you change a world

    • Back up the world first. This tool writes a new file and never touches your original, but a backup costs nothing.
    • Experiments permanently mark a world. Minecraft records that experiments were used. Turning them all off here removes those markers, but the game may re-add them.
    • Not every feature exists in every build. Enabling a flag for something your version does not contain can crash the game or damage the save.
    • Minecraft Education hides this toggle on purpose. On a school-managed account this may cut against your IT policy — check before relying on it for classwork.
