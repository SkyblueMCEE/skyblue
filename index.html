What is SKYBLUE

Tools that run in your browser

  • Works with Education and Bedrock. Same world format, same tools — set up a classroom activity or edit your own survival world.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your world is read and rewritten in this tab. You can turn off your wifi and the tools still work.
  • No install, no account. Useful on school-managed devices where you cannot install software.
  • Open source. Every tool's code is public, so you can check for yourself that nothing leaves your device.

SKYBLUE runs entirely in your browser. No uploads, no accounts, no tracking, no cookies.

Built on the Minecraft Bedrock community's NBT and level.dat documentation. Not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.
